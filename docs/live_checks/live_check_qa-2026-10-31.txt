[answerable] How does compound interest work?
  status:    ok
  rewritten: compound interest formula investment growth calculation
  answer:    Compound interest means earning interest on both your original amount and the interest already earned. So, over time, interest can begin earning interest of its own. [1][2]

For example, if ₹10,000 grows by 8% in a year, it becomes ₹10,800. The next year’s 8% is calculated on ₹10,800, not just the original ₹10,000. [3]
