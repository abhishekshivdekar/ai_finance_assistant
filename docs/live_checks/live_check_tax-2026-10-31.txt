======== Question: Which rate applies to equity fund gains held for 2 years? ========
status: ok

======== Question: How does PPF work? ========
status: ok

PPF is a government-backed savings account for individuals. You deposit money into it and earn interest declared by the government. [1] [2]

The interest is not linked to share prices, and its rate is reviewed periodically, so it can change. [2] The account has a long lock-in period. [1]

For advice about your own situation, consult a chartered accountant.

======== Question: What is STT? ========
status: ok

STT, or securities transaction tax, is a tax collected on certain transactions in listed securities on recognised stock exchanges. It is separate from tax on the profit itself. [1]

======== Question: What is the difference between PPF and NPS? ========
status: ok

PPF is a government-backed savings scheme: you deposit money and earn interest at a rate declared by the government, rather than returns tied to the stock market. [1] NPS is market-linked: its investments may include shares and bonds, so what you receive depends on how they perform. [2] Part of the NPS amount collected at retirement must be turned into regular income. [2]

This is general education, not personal tax advice. Rules can change; consult a chartered accountant about your own situation. [3]
